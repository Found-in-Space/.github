# Community conduct

Found in Space welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience, and
ways of participating. These standards apply in the project's issues, pull
requests, and other project-managed community spaces, and when representing
the project publicly.

## Working together

- Treat people with respect. Welcome questions and make room for beginners.
- Critique ideas, evidence, and code constructively. Explain disagreements and
  be willing to revise a conclusion when the evidence changes.
- Respect people's identity, background, ability, boundaries, and privacy.
- Give credit, acknowledge uncertainty, and help others participate.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual harassment, sustained personal
attacks, and publishing private information without permission are not
acceptable. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern is also unacceptable.

## Reporting a concern

Contact [found-in-space@k-si.com](mailto:found-in-space@k-si.com) privately.
Include the relevant links and context, and tell us if there are constraints on
how you can safely be contacted. You do not need to confront the person involved
or publish the incident in an issue.

Reports will be handled with care for the privacy and safety of those involved.
Information may need to be shared to understand or address a report; absolute
confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. GitHub's own reporting and blocking tools
are also available for concerns on its platform.

## Moderation

Project maintainers may ask for a change in behaviour, edit or remove
inappropriate contributions, or restrict participation temporarily or
permanently. The response should take account of the conduct, its impact, and
any repeated behaviour. Apply these standards fairly, including to maintainers.
